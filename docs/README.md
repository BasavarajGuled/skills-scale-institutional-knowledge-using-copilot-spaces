# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A concise overview of OctoAcme's project management processes, with links to the full guidance.

## Project management processes

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative project lifecycle. Teams initiate work by validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and defining measurable success criteria. Approved initiatives move into planning, where teams break work into shippable increments, prioritize and estimate backlog items, agree on milestones and a Definition of Done, and record risks and dependencies. During execution, teams track work on a project board, coordinate through regular check-ins, review and test small changes, monitor delivery signals, and escalate blockers. Releases follow readiness checks, deployment verification, and rollback planning. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents turn lessons into owned, measurable improvements. Clear role ownership and regular stakeholder communication support every stage.
